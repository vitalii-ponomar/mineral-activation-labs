Service structure

Focused support for early-stage technical decisions.

The aim is not to create a long list of generic consultancy tasks. It is to offer sharp technical help where feedstock uncertainty is blocking progress.

Feedstock intelligence

Review what the material is likely to be, which comparable systems matter, and where the biggest uncertainty currently sits.

Reactivity and material potential

Identify whether the material behaves as reactive, supplementary, inert-but-useful, or only conditionally attractive.

Application pathway selection

Shortlist the routes with the strongest technical logic and the lowest strategic noise.

Next-step programme design

Recommend the shortest set of tests and analyses needed to support a serious go/no-go decision.

How engagements usually progress

A cleaner path from material review to technical direction.

1. Review the materialAvailable chemistry, mineralogy, process background, and commercial context.
2. Screen the fundamentalsWhat the feedstock probably is, what it is not, and what is most likely to matter.
3. Define the routeSelect the strongest product logic instead of running too many weak possibilities in parallel.
4. Recommend next stepsTesting plan, collaboration direction, pilot logic, or a justified stop decision.
Possible deliverables
  • Feedstock qualification memoTechnical attractiveness, risks, and likely development routes.
  • Application shortlistPrioritised pathway options with rationale.
  • Testing roadmapLean programme focused on answers, not on volume of data.
  • Partner-ready framingSharper material story for internal teams, collaborators, and customers.
Best fit

Ideal when the material looks promising, but the pathway is still unclear.
