Core advisory capabilities

Focused technical support for turning mineral streams into realistic product and market directions.

Feedstock review and application fit

We evaluate whether a material can enter an existing product pathway without activation, and where the strongest commercial fit is likely to sit first.

  • Review of existing site, laboratory, and production data
  • Screening against likely applications such as aggregate, SCM, fill, stabilisation, bricks, or related mineral products
  • Translation of mineralogy, chemistry, PSD, and contaminants into development relevance
  • Early ranking of the most credible application pathways

Outcome: a decision-ready view of where the material is most likely to fit first.

Standards and compliance positioning

We compare available material data with the technical intent of relevant AS, NZS, and ASTM frameworks to identify current compliance, likely compliance, and the main gaps.

  • Review against aggregate, SCM, binder, soil improvement, and product-relevant standards
  • Identification of properties already supporting compliance
  • Gap analysis for properties that still require testing or refinement
  • Practical view on whether compliance is realistic without activation

Outcome: a clear picture of standards fit, risk, and what evidence is still missing.

Pathway development and technical de-risking

Where direct use is not strong enough, we define the most credible route to improve fit, performance, and product value.

  • Selection of the most justified testing sequence for the chosen application
  • Targeted interpretation of why a material passes, underperforms, or fails
  • Recommendation of activation, beneficiation, blending, calcination, washing, or classification routes only where justified
  • Guidance from early product testing toward scale-up priorities and market-facing direction

Outcome: a technically credible route from material screening to product development.

How a material becomes a viable product

The workflow is structured around decisions: use existing evidence first, identify the strongest non-activated route, position it against standards, close only the critical gaps, then validate the chosen product pathway before treatment or scale-up.

Root decision

Start with the material as it already exists

Use existing site data, internal lab results, process history, and environmental evidence first. Only add new work where it improves the quality of the decision.

Product gate

Validate the chosen product route

Test the shortlisted material inside the selected product system and assess performance against standards at finished-product level, not just at feedstock level.

Pathway A · works

Proceed to scale-up and market use

If the route performs strongly, we define scale-up priorities, standards-aligned positioning, and the most promising compliant end-use or client segments.

Pathway B · needs refinement

Apply the simplest justified treatment route

If performance falls short, we recommend the most defensible upgrade path, such as blending, beneficiation, activation, calcination, washing, grinding, or classification, then re-check the improved route.
