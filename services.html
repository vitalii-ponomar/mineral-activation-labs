Services

Core advisory capabilities

Mineral Activation Labs supports organisations working with mineral resources and industrial side streams that may have value in low-carbon construction materials. The work is built around three practical questions: is the material worth developing, what is technically driving its behaviour, and what pathway could make it viable?

Typical engagement outcomes

Clearer go / no-go decisionsReduce wasted testing on unrealistic pathways.
Sharper test plansDefine what should be measured next and why.
More credible project directionMove from scattered data to a practical technical pathway.

Feedstock intelligence

Assess whether a mineral stream represents a credible technical opportunity, including clays, slags, soils, tailings, fines, and mineral residues.

Reactivity and pathway definition

Determine whether the most realistic route is calcination, blending, activation, carbonation, stabilisation, ceramics, or selective use.

Mechanism-focused troubleshooting

Resolve why a material is underperforming, inconsistent, or difficult to scale using mineralogical and reaction-based interpretation.

What each engagement may include

The exact scope depends on the question, but most projects combine material screening with a clearer view of pathway fit and technical risk.

Material and data review

  • Existing chemistry, mineralogy, and process data review
  • Feedstock variability and impurity-risk interpretation
  • Gap identification before broader testing

Pathway framing

  • Likely fit in SCM, binder, carbonation, ceramic, or civil pathways
  • Processing demand and scale-up implications
  • Priority questions before pilot-stage work

Technical test planning

  • Sharper test sequences aligned with mechanism
  • Focused lab work instead of broad exploratory programmes
  • Decision-ready interpretation of results

Applied scientific interpretation

  • Phase assemblage and reaction-pathway analysis
  • Interface, gel, or durability bottleneck interpretation
  • Translation of scientific data into project direction

How projects usually start

Most enquiries begin with a real material, an existing dataset, or a product question that has become technically unclear. From there, the goal is to narrow uncertainty quickly and define the next step with better technical confidence.
