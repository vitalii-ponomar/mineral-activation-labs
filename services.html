Core services

Mineral Activation Labs is not a general testing provider. We offer focused technical advisory for organisations trying to determine whether a mineral side-stream has real development potential, what is technically driving its behaviour, and which pathway is most credible to pursue.

Feedstock qualification

We assess whether a mineral resource, industrial residue, or excavated material is genuinely worth advancing. The emphasis is on technical quality, development relevance, and early separation of promising materials from those unlikely to justify further effort.

Reactivity evaluation

We evaluate whether a material is likely to perform as a supplementary cementitious material, alkali-activated precursor, reactive mineral component, or related low-carbon binder input — and under which conditions that potential is likely to become usable.

Mineralogical interpretation

We translate mineralogical, chemical, and thermal data into practical technical meaning. This helps distinguish apparent opportunity based on bulk composition from real opportunity based on reactive phases, constraints, and likely behaviour in a formulated system.

Activation and process strategy

We identify realistic technical routes such as calcination, grinding, classification, blending, alkaline activation, washing, or stabilisation, always in relation to the actual feedstock and the value of the target application.

Performance-oriented test planning

We define the most useful next-stage testing pathway to answer the commercial and technical question at hand, whether that means screening reactivity, comparing process options, or building an evidence base for pilot-stage decisions.

Technical decision support

We bring the work together into a clear technical view: what is promising, what is limiting, what should be tested next, and what should be deprioritised. The goal is not more data alone, but better development decisions.

What each service can include

Each engagement is scoped around a decision, not a generic package. Depending on the material and the opportunity, the work may combine screening, interpretation, pathway definition, and targeted technical recommendations.

Feedstock qualification and screening

  • Review of available chemistry, mineralogy, site context, and process background
  • Assessment of likely reactive phases, inert fractions, and undesirable components
  • Identification of contaminants, heterogeneity, and practical development risks
  • Initial judgement on suitability for cementitious or related mineral applications
  • Priority ranking or early go / no-go view across one or multiple materials

Reactivity and application relevance

  • Assessment of pozzolanic, latent hydraulic, or alkali-activation potential
  • Interpretation of which phases are likely to contribute to reaction and performance
  • Consideration of thermal activation potential for clays, soils, and fine mineral streams
  • Comparison of candidate pathways for SCM use, activated binders, or blended systems
  • Identification of the main technical unknowns that should be resolved next

Mineralogical interpretation and technical insight

  • Translation of XRD, XRF, thermal, and related data into development-relevant conclusions
  • Distinction between strong bulk chemistry and truly useful reactive mineral content
  • Interpretation of how mineralogy may influence processing demand, variability, and product fit
  • Clarification of why apparently similar materials can behave very differently in practice
  • Framing of opportunity around realistic technical mechanisms rather than generic claims

Processing, activation, and next-step strategy

  • Review of likely needs for grinding, classification, washing, drying, calcination, or blending
  • Assessment of whether thermal or chemical activation is technically justified
  • Matching of process intensity to the probable value of the end application
  • Definition of the most useful next laboratory, validation, or pilot-oriented tests
