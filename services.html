Core services

We support mineral feedstock development from early screening through to performance-focused technical guidance.

Feedstock qualification

Assess chemistry, mineralogy, contaminants, and variability of mineral side-streams to determine whether they are technically worth advancing.

Reactivity assessment

Evaluate whether a material shows pozzolanic, alkali-activation, or other reactive potential relevant to low-carbon binder systems.

Mineralogical interpretation

Translate mineralogical and microstructural data into practical insights about likely behaviour, limitations, and product fit.

Activation strategy development

Identify technically realistic pathways such as calcination, alkaline activation, blending, classification, or stabilisation.

Material performance testing

Define and assess relevant testing pathways for strength, reaction behaviour, durability indicators, and early product feasibility.

Strategic resource assessment

Help prioritise deposits, residues, or stockpiled materials based on technical promise, likely constraints, and development logic.

What each service can include

The exact scope depends on the material and the decision the client needs to make, but these are the typical elements.

Feedstock qualification and interpretation

  • Review of available chemistry and mineralogy data
  • Assessment of likely reactive phases and inert fractions
  • Identification of impurities, contaminants, or variability risks
  • Initial view on technical suitability and likely constraints

Reactivity and binder relevance

  • Pozzolanic and dissolution-oriented assessment logic
  • Potential for alkali activation or thermal activation
  • Preliminary relevance for low-cement or alternative binder systems
  • Comparison of candidate material pathways

Processing and activation pathways

  • Calcination pathway suitability
  • Grinding, classification, washing, or blending needs
  • Alignment between processing demand and target application
  • Practical next-step testing recommendations

Performance-focused technical direction

  • Definition of the most useful next laboratory tests
  • Guidance for pilot-oriented material evaluation
  • Early no-go or prioritisation decisions where appropriate
  • Clear summary of what is promising and what is blocking progress

How projects usually start

Most projects begin with a short technical review of the available material data. From there, we identify realistic pathways and the most useful next tests.

1

Share material information

Provide chemistry, mineralogy, site context, or a short description of the feedstock and the opportunity being considered.

2

Initial technical screening

We review the material through the lens of mineral phases, reactivity, contaminants, processing demand, and possible end uses.

3

Define realistic pathways

We identify plausible activation, blending, or stabilisation routes and where the strongest technical case may sit.

4

Recommend next steps

The output is a practical direction for targeted testing, deeper evaluation, or an early no-go decision.
