Project themes

Projects that convert technical uncertainty into clearer material strategy.

The strongest portfolio pages do not just list research topics. They show the kinds of mineral problems already being tackled and the quality of thinking behind them.

What this page proves
  • Range across feedstock classesClays, soils, slags, byproducts, and carbon-linked systems.
  • Strong pathway thinkingProjects connect mineral behaviour to application logic.
  • Real-world relevanceThemes that map onto deployment, processing, and product questions.
Treated soils

Low-cement construction materials from treated and clay-rich soils

Assessment of reactivity, blend design, and performance potential for soils that would otherwise remain low-value or difficult to place.

Fe-rich byproducts

Fe-rich slags and difficult metallurgical residues

Evaluation of activation behaviour, phase evolution, and product fit in mineral systems many groups avoid because they are hard to interpret.

Carbon-linked systems

Carbonation–hydration pathways for secondary mineral resources

Routes where curing strategy, mineral composition, and reaction pathway interact to create both performance value and carbon benefit.

Fired products

Excavated soils and waste-derived ceramic products

Development concepts for turning low-value soil-like streams into fired products with stronger structural or decorative potential.

Clay opportunity mapping

Calcined clay screening and resource qualification

Identifying which clay resources justify calcination and where their performance potential sits within practical SCM development.

Mechanistic binder science

Interface-controlled performance in silicate-rich systems

Work that explains how interfacial chemistry and microstructure govern strength and durability in non-traditional binder systems.
