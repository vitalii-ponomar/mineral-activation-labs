Applications

Core application categories

Mineral side streams can support a range of construction and mineral-based products. The most suitable pathway depends on mineralogy, chemistry, reactivity, processing demand, and performance requirements.

Pathway principle

Not every feedstock belongs in the same product. The right value often comes from choosing the application that matches the material rather than forcing the material into a preferred category.

Supplementary cementitious materials

Selected mineral resources can partially replace Portland cement where chemistry, amorphous content, and fineness support useful pozzolanic or latent-hydraulic behaviour.

Alkali-activated and alternative binders

Some mineral streams are better suited to activated systems than to direct cement replacement because dissolution behaviour and activator compatibility matter more than simple oxide tables.

Low-cement construction materials

Useful where the objective is clinker reduction with practical performance in blends, mortars, controlled low-strength materials, or engineered construction products.

Stabilisation and engineered fill

Large-volume civil use can be the strongest route for materials that do not justify higher-intensity processing or premium-performance binder applications.

Fired mineral products

Some clay-rich or mineral-rich materials are more credible in bricks, ceramic products, lightweight aggregates, or thermally stabilised products than in cementitious pathways.

Carbonation-related products

Some calcium- or magnesium-bearing materials can support carbonated mineral products and CO₂-reactive construction materials when the reaction pathway is appropriate.

How application pathways are identified

Selecting the right application requires linking material properties with realistic processing and performance targets.

01
Mineralogy controls reactivity and likely phase evolution.
02
Chemistry shapes binder formation, impurities, and environmental fit.
03
Processing demand decides whether a route remains realistic.
04
Deployment context determines whether the product can actually scale.
