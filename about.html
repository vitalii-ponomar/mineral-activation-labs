Research-linked foundation

Mineral Activation Labs is closely linked to research activity at UNSW Sydney, including work connected to the School of Civil and Environmental Engineering. Its scientific basis includes low-carbon cementitious systems, alkali activation, silicate chemistry, mineral feedstock reactivity, and the technical behaviour of industrial side streams.

Research themes

  • Low-carbon cement and concrete materials
  • Alkali-activated and sodium silicate systems
  • Calcined clays and mineral reactivity
  • Fe-rich byproducts and metallurgical residues
