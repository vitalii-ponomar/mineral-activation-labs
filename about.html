About

Scientific depth with industry-facing direction

Mineral Activation Labs brings together expertise across civil engineering, mineralogy, geochemistry, and cementitious materials to evaluate underused mineral resources for low-carbon construction materials and related mineral products.

Positioning

The platform sits between scientific depth and applied development, helping partners determine whether a mineral stream is technically worth developing and under what pathway.

Core technical expertise

Mineralogical interpretation, reaction-driven chemistry, processing realism, and performance relevance in engineered material systems.

Scientific background

Research-linked capability connected to low-carbon cementitious systems, alkali-activated materials, sodium silicate chemistry, calcined clays, and difficult mineral streams.

Industry-facing relevance

Support before broader testing or pilot work begins, when technical uncertainty is still high and material direction is not yet clear.

Team

Mineral Activation Labs is supported by a multidisciplinary scientific team with backgrounds across civil engineering, mineralogy, geochemistry, cement and concrete materials, and waste-derived mineral systems.

  • Capability in cementitious systems, silicate binders, alkali activation, phase evolution, and material performance.
  • Expertise in interpreting feedstocks through phase assemblage, transformation behaviour, and likely technical constraints.
  • Team capability focused on turning scientific data into clearer decisions.

Research-linked foundation

The scientific basis is informed by active research linked to UNSW Sydney and broader collaborations across mineral resources and low-carbon construction materials.
